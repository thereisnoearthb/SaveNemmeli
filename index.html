  • The Kovalam-Nemmeli coastal wetland, a tidal wetland near Chennai, is a biodiversity refuge and a haven for resident and migratory birds. Over 12,000 fisherfolk, many of them women who hand-gather prawns, crabs, and oysters from the shallow backwaters, depend on the bountiful marshlands for their livelihood.

  • The Rs. 342.60 crore Mamallan Reservoir threatens to turn more than 5,000 acres of the marshlands into a freshwater reservoir by blocking the sea from entering the salt marshes.

  • This is a dangerous idea: It will not only destroy the saline habitat for diverse lifeforms and rob local communities of their livelihoods, it will also expose the coast to stormsurges and tidal flooding, a risk that technical reviews of the project have failed to adequately address.
